What is the Shepard Tone?

A Shepard Tone is an acoustic illusion — multiple octaves of the same note layered together, with higher octaves fading out as lower ones fade in. To the brain, the pitch sounds like it rises forever with no ceiling and no resolution. This version pairs that endless rising sensation with the ascending fractal for a truly infinite experience.
